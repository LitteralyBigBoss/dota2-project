Gameplay Changes

Key Mechanics — Patch 7.41

Structural changes affecting every game from draft to GG.

🗺️

Map Geometry Overhaul

Both side lanes received cliff and tree adjustments that open new high-ground ward spots and alter smoke paths significantly.

  • NEWSecondary rune spawns added to river
  • BUFFRadiant jungle camp stacking improved
  • NERFDire offlane safe zone reduced
⚡

Neutral Creep Rework

Neutral item drop rates adjusted at all tiers. Tier 1 items drop 25% more frequently, creating stronger early snowball potential.

  • BUFFTier 1 drop rate: 14% → 18%
  • NERFTier 3 drop rate: 10% → 8%
  • NEW3 new Tier 2 neutral items added
🏰

Tower Changes

Towers across all tiers received armor and damage adjustments to reduce early tower diving and extend the laning phase.

  • BUFFT1 tower armor: 5 → 8
  • NERFT2 tower bounty gold reduced 12%
  • BUFFOutpost vision range extended
🐉

Roshan Adjustments

Roshan's pit location slightly shifted toward the river. Spawn timers standardized. Cheese drop rate on third kill increased.

  • NEWRoshan pit repositioned
  • BUFFCheese drop: 50% → 65%
  • NERFBase HP: 6500 → 6200
🌿

Bounty Rune Economy

Bounty rune gold scaling adjusted per minute. Early game economy shifts favor aggressive trilane setups and invading jungles.

  • BUFFMin 0–5 bounty gold +15
  • NERFLate-game bounty scaling nerfed
  • BUFFShared gold radius increased
🎯

Spell Immunity Changes

Several abilities that previously pierced BKB no longer do so. This heavily buffs carry-oriented metas and reduces support dominance.

  • NERF6 abilities removed from BKB pierce
  • BUFFBKB duration: 5s (lvl 1) restored
  • NEWSpell lifestealer passive reworked
